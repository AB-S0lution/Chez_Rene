# Chez René (Bistrot Chez René) — Research & Plan

> **Chez René** is a *bistrot de quartier* with a bar and cocktail bar at **6 boulevard René Laënnec, 35000 Rennes**, near Saint-Hélier. It cooks homemade, seasonal bistro food from a short menu that changes every week. Lunch is a €21 three-course formula and dinner costs €25–30 for three courses. There is no wine list: guests get up and choose a bottle in the *espace cave*, "comme à la maison". The bar serves signature cocktails, mocktails, a gin & tonic range and seasonal craft beers. The venue is a row of rooms *en enfilade*: high tables at the bar, bistro tables, cushioned alcoves and a back room "esprit pub anglais revisité", furnished with vintage pieces collected over time and lit low ("ambiance feutrée"). It hosts blind tests, karaoke and cocktail nights. It opens Monday to Friday only (lunch and late evening). It is run by KARIOCA BSB (gérant Nicolas Potier). From Dec 2023 until about Sept 2025 it traded as **"Les Bricoles"**, and the website still uses that old domain. What sets it apart: "pas de chichi", prices customers call "rikiki", a personal welcome they praise by name, and about 4.9/5 on every review platform. The name honours René Laennec, inventor of the stethoscope, whose name is on the boulevard's street signs (Ouest-France, 23/09/2025). Chez René was one of the selected finalists of the 2026 Championnat de Bretagne de l'œuf mayonnaise (ASOM).

## 2. Story & history

**The premises at 6 bd René Laënnec have long been a neighbourhood bar.** Sources: OSM node history, BODACC, register.
- **Until Nov 2017:** a bar-restaurant run by a sole trader, on the register for this address from 2000 to 2017. The name is withheld because the owner was a private individual.
- **Nov 2017 – 2020/21: Ch'Ty Bar** (CH'TY COMPANY). Business bought for €105,000. A "bistrot moderne" with Belgian beers, a house "Ch'Tyramisu" and evening tapas. https://www.chtybar.com/
- **2020 – Nov 2023: L'Atypik, "bistrot culturel et solidaire".** A cooperative (SCIC) created 2020-10-21, with concerts, exhibitions and talks. Unidivers (30 Nov 2020, **re-checked in the critic pass**) gives 42 associates and founders David Bizière and François Frenod, and describes the space as « 100 m² en enfilade où quatre salles se succèdent, tout en longueur »; David said it « rappelle cette idée de péniche ». **Capacity then: designed for 45 seats** (24 under Covid rules). This is the only seat figure found for these premises; it predates the Chez René fit-out, so treat it as context, not as Chez René's capacity. https://unidivers.fr/atypik-bistro-culturel-solidaire-rennes/
- **21 Dec 2023: Les Bricoles.** KARIOCA BSB bought the business from L'Atypik for €150,000. It ran as a "bistrot & bar à manger" inspired by Parisian *bouillons*, with sharing plates and French tapas in the evening and DJ sets at weekends (archived site). OSM renamed the node "Les bricoles" on 2023-12-25. The Les Bricoles-era bar page (Wayback, 11 Sept 2025) differed from today's in three ways worth knowing: the bar was "ouvert en continu toute la journée", guests could "réserver une table pour boire un verre" with table service ("le service ne se fait pas au comptoir"), and gins were also served "en collaboration avec la marque Infuse Me". **All three lines were dropped from the Chez René copy; do not reuse them.** The site's rating widget then read "4.8 / 5 basé sur 42 avis" (now 4.9 on 229). http://web.archive.org/web/20250911154534/https://www.les-bricoles-restaurant-rennes.fr/le-bar-les-bricoles-restaurant-rennes
- **About Sept 2025: Chez René**, a "vrai bistrot" de quartier with a menu that changes every week. The site now says explicitly: "L'établissement n'est pas un bouillon mais un vrai bistrot". This is a deliberate shift away from the Les Bricoles bouillon / tapas concept.

**The owner's earlier venture** (public records only):
- Nicolas Potier is also gérant of **SARL LES BRICOLES** (SIREN 815 356 209), created 2016-01-02.
- In Jan 2016 that company bought the bar-restaurant at **17 quai de la Prévalaye** from ALBANOV, which had traded as "Café des Bricoles" from 2004 to 2015, for €248,000. The Prévalaye Les Bricoles was known for French tapas, signature cocktails, gin & tonics and brunch.
- Feb 2021: the company moved its head office to 11 rue Victor Hugo, where it ran "L'Épicerie des Bricoles" (an épicerie fine).
- Aug 2022: the Prévalaye business was sold to MAISON JAVA for €440,000. The new owner kept a "Les Bricoles" there; it is now shown as closed.
- Nov 2023: Potier's new company, KARIOCA BSB, took over 6 bd Laënnec and reused the Les Bricoles name, concept and website domain.
- **The gin, cocktail and "bar à manger" DNA therefore runs through the whole Les Bricoles lineage into Chez René.**
- ⚠ **Never mention on the site:** SARL Les Bricoles (815 356 209, *not* KARIOCA BSB) was put into judicial liquidation on 2025-11-05 (BODACC 2025-11-14). This is noted only so nobody confuses the two companies. Also not attributable to Chez René: a Pernod-Ricard 2020–21 report naming Alexandra Potier and Astrid Dorlencourt as partners of the *old* Les Bricoles.
- The story "it moved from quai de la Prévalaye" (Ouest-France) is loose wording. The Prévalaye site was sold in Aug 2022, more than a year before Laënnec opened.

**Why "René"? (now confirmed by press; critic pass read the full article body)**
- **Ouest-France, « À Rennes, Chez René ravive l'ambiance du bistrot de quartier à des prix modérés », by Pascal Simon, published 23/09/2025 at 17:50.** Standfirst: « Connu longtemps sous le nom Les Bricoles, ce restaurant du boulevard René-Laënnec change d'identité pour offrir une cuisine de bistrot, alliant simplicité et prix maîtrisés. » https://www.ouest-france.fr/bretagne/rennes-35000/a-rennes-chez-rene-ravive-lambiance-du-bistrot-de-quartier-5b846c4e-97bd-11f0-aae5-c246857d365e

## 6. Place & context

- **Street:** boulevard René Laënnec runs roughly north–south on the east side of the centre. No. 6 is at the northern end, about **50 m from Lycée Laënnec** (Charles Tillon site). The boulevard runs south to the Vilaine and the Saint-Hélier bridge.
- **Quartier:** Saint-Hélier / La Petite Californie (Thabor – Saint-Hélier – Alphonse Guérin).
  - It was a working-class faubourg after the 1720 fire, home to mill, printing and biscuit-factory workers.
  - The riverbanks got the nickname **"La Petite Californie"** from property speculation before the railway arrived in 1857.
  - About 3,738 residents (average age 36, 71% renters) and about 90 local shops. Examples: the Maison Coupel bakery and the 80-year-old Lemercier hardware store.
  - Described as having a "village atmosphere". Source: estate-agent pages, medium confidence.
  - Links: https://www.rennesimmo9.com/actualites/investissement/quartier-saint-helier-rennes · https://edito.seloger.com/conseils-d-experts/acheter/rennes-meilleurs-quartiers-vivre-famille-article-19425.html · https://ryo.co/fr/articles/france/rennes/quartiers-rennes/
- **Landmarks** (straight-line distances):
  - Alphonse.G bistrot about 130 m
  - **TNB** (1 rue Saint-Hélier; a former military prison where Dreyfus was held in 1899) about 530 m
  - **Parc du Thabor** about 570 m
  - Pont Saint-Hélier / the Vilaine about 580 m
  - **Piscine Saint-Georges** (Odorico mosaics) about 710 m
  - **La Brasserie** (former Graff / Kronenbourg brewery) about 750 m
  - Les Champs Libres about 820 m
  - Le Liberté about 870 m
  - **Gare de Rennes / EuroRennes** about 880 m
  - République about 1.0 km
  - **Place des Lices** (Saturday market) about 1.3 km
- **Brewing heritage on the doorstep.** The Graff brewery was founded on rue Saint-Hélier in 1872, bought by Kronenbourg in 1986 and closed in 2003. It reopened in Oct 2021 as "La Brasserie", with a microbrewery serving a beer "made in Saint-Hélier". https://www.lejournaldesentreprises.com/article/lancienne-usine-kronenbourg-devient-le-nouveau-temple-de-la-rse-rennaise-1652761 · https://unidivers.fr/brasserie-saint-helier-rennes/
- **Odorico mosaics:**
  - The Italian mosaicist family worked in Rennes from 1882 to 1978. About 47 sites survive.
  - Styles range from Art Nouveau (red marble, flowers, waves on green-to-yellow grounds) to Art Deco. The Saint-Georges pool is teal and gold.
  - Saint-Hélier has an **Odorico footbridge**.
  - The venue has a **round mosaic mirror**. This is our strongest local design bridge.
  - https://www.lalibre.be/culture/arts/2018/08/11/odorico-lart-deco-rennais-K5255OYJKJE7ZEPROFBJE7UAKY/ · https://www.tourismebretagne.com/selon-mes-envies/topitos/4-escales-ou-la-culture-fait-des-etincelles/
- **Timber-framed houses:** 286 coloured *maisons à pans de bois*, with lozenge and cross-bracing patterns. Use them only as rhythm or line patterns. https://www.techno-science.net/fr/definitions/patrimoine-architectural-de-rennes
- **Gallo, not Breton:**
  - Rennes is in Haute-Bretagne, where the historic regional language is Gallo (about 132,000 speakers in 2024).
  - A word of Gallo in microcopy is more authentic than Breton signage. **Verify any Gallo word with Chubri before use.**
  - https://www.bretagne.bzh/app/uploads/24-CP-Etude-sociolinguistique-2024.pdf · https://www.lexilogos.com/gallo_dictionnaire.htm · https://www.chubri-galo.bzh
- **Music city:** the Trans Musicales since 1979, plus "Bars en Trans". These frame the venue's theme nights well. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rencontres_Trans_Musicales · https://www.lonelyplanet.com/articles/france-rencontres-trans-musicales-guide
- **Marché des Lices:** every Saturday since 1622, about 250 producers. There is **no evidence that Chez René buys there.** https://lebonbon.fr/rennes/news/deuxieme-plus-grand-marche-france-se-trouve-a-rennes-depuis-400-ans/ · https://www.routard.com/fr/guide/france/bretagne/rennes/marche-des-lices
- **Local products** that already appear on the menu: cocos de Paimpol, lieu noir breton, sarrasin, cidre. Other regional products (coucou de Rennes, beurre demi-sel, galette-saucisse) are context only and **not** on the menu. https://www.ouigo.com/blog/specialites-culinaires-rennes · https://www.reussir.fr/volailles/la-coucou-de-rennes-se-raconte
- **Avoid:** Breton stripes, triskels, Gwenn-ha-Du flags, lighthouses, coastal blue, bigoudènes, crêpe clichés. Rennes is inland and urban, and this is a Paris-style bistro.
- **Who passes the door:** weekday office workers (EuroRennes and the boulevards), lycée staff, TNB and Champs Libres staff at lunch; residents in their 30s and theatre-goers in the evening; station users. Few tourists. This mix is inferred, medium confidence.

## 9. Design direction

**Skills consulted:** `ui-ux-pro-max:ui-ux-pro-max` (design-system search) and `design-taste-frontend`.
- **Rejected:** ui-ux-pro-max's automatic output. It returned "Minimalism & Swiss Style" with SaaS blue #2563EB and orange, a generic match that ignores this venue.
- **Kept from it:** its restaurant type pairing hint (Playfair Display SC + Karla; adapted below), the GSAP Flip page-transition idea and its checklist.
- **Design read** (design-taste §0.B): *Reading this as a complete multi-page site for a neighbourhood bistro-bar, for local office workers at lunch and couples or friends in the evening. The visual language is vintage velvet-and-cane, "bistro at night", built on native CSS + Tailwind v4 + GSAP, with type set like the real logo.*
- **Dials:** VARIANCE 7 · MOTION 8 · DENSITY 4.

### Concept: **« L'Enfilade »**
**One-line idea:** the website is the venue walked front to back, door after door: from the *comptoir* at noon to the *espace cave* at one in the morning. It is lit by the house's own fringed lamp and switches between **Midi** and **Soir**.

The idea comes straight from the business:
- The rooms really are "en enfilade".
- There really are two menus, Midi and Soir, with two time slots.
- Guests really get up and walk to the cave to choose their wine.

**Personality keywords:** feutré · chaleureux · gourmand · sans chichi · vintage chiné · vivant · velours · de quartier.

### Palette

**Why not the "AI warm cream + brass" default:**
- The cream here is the real logo colour, and the cognac is the real banquette velvet. That meets design-taste's override condition: a genuinely vintage brand, and every colour is articulated.
- The dominant colour is the **velvet blue**, which no other site in this portfolio uses.
- **Only one accent (cognac), locked across the whole site.**

**Theme:** Soir (dark) is the default and follows `prefers-color-scheme: dark`. Midi is light, with a crème ground and velvet-ombre text. The lamp switch (below) is the one deliberate theme change on the site, which design-taste §4.11 allows as a single strong device.

### Type pairing
- **Display: Bodoni Moda** (Google Fonts, has an opsz axis). It is a high-contrast didone, the closest free match to the logo's tall serif capitals.
  - A serif is justified here: the logo literally *is* a didone and the brand is vintage.
  - Use it for page titles, the wordmark, dish names on the ardoise and big numbers (prices).
  - Italics are allowed for emphasis in the same family. Keep at least 1.1 line-height for descenders (p, g in « goûter »).
- **Labels: Josefin Sans**, capitals with wide tracking, mirroring the logo's geometric sans arc text ("BISTROT · DE QUARTIER"). Josefin Sans is already loaded on the current site. Use it sparingly: arc text, badges, nav, at most one eyebrow per three sections.
- **Body: Karla** (ui-ux-pro-max's restaurant pairing). Humanist-grotesque, readable at 16–18 px on dark.
- **No handwriting or chalk font**, even for the ardoise. The ardoise is set in Bodoni Moda italic, revealed with a chalk-wipe mask.
- Self-host via `@fontsource` packages (check package names at build time); `font-display: swap`.

### Imagery (we may NOT use the Novy Event photos)
Build original CSS / SVG / canvas art from the real room vocabulary, in one consistent fine-line, flat-plus-texture style:
- **Velvet:** SVG `feTurbulence` noise over radial gradients of #384870 → #181828, with a soft sheen that follows the pointer.
- **Cannage:** cane-weave pattern as a CSS `repeating-conic-gradient` or SVG pattern. Used as a button hover fill, as dividers and as the background of chair-back frames that hold content.
- **Gilded sunburst mirrors:** an SVG radial-ray mirror that frames the hero wordmark and reflects a blurred "room".
- **Round mosaic mirror and Odorico tesserae:** section dividers whose tesserae assemble on scroll; a mosaic monogram "R" in an oval for the favicon and loader.
- **Fringed floral lampshade:** the light source and the Midi/Soir switch.
- **Top-down plate illustrations** for signature dishes: green œuf mayo, a terrine slice, mousse au chocolat with fleur de sel flakes, côte de bœuf. Simple shapes, on white oval plates on a wine-red ground (from their photo style).

- **The street-name plaque (critic pass, grounded in the press):** the name comes from René Laennec « mentionné sur les plaques de rue du boulevard ». A street-plaque lockup reading "BOULEVARD RENÉ LAENNEC" can carry the "Pourquoi René ?" story, the footer address and the 404 page. ### Signature interaction: « Allez choisir votre bouteille » (the cave wall)
This is unique to Chez René: there is no wine list, and guests get up and pick a bottle.
- A full-width **wooden cave shelf** of mock bottles (SVG silhouettes, tinted by colour: rouge / blanc / rosé / orange / bulles).
- **Filters:** nature · biodynamie · traditionnel; light ↔ structured.
- **Interaction:**
  - The user drags the shelf (inertia) or uses the arrow keys.
  - Hover lifts a bottle (spring).
  - Click **pulls it off the shelf** with a GSAP Flip to a table at the front. Its label flips over into a card: mock style, grapes, region, "avec quoi ?" pairing with real dishes from §3 (for example, with the onglet).
  - **Real copy:** « Côté vins, pas de carte imposée… à vous de vous lever et d'aller choisir directement dans notre espace cave, comme à la maison. »
  - **Mock data, clearly labelled.** No real prices; the copy says « Demandez conseil à l'équipe ».

**Secondary signature: the lamp switch Midi / Soir.**
- A pull-cord on the fringed lampshade in the header.
- Pulling it (click, Enter or Space) runs a **View Transition with a circular reveal from the lamp's position**.
- It swaps the theme tokens, swaps the menu shown (Carte du Midi ↔ Carte du Soir) and highlights the matching hours slot.
- By default the site picks the mode from the visitor's local time (Midi before 17:00) and also respects `prefers-color-scheme`. The choice is stored in `localStorage` (try/catch).

### Motion language
Stack: GSAP + ScrollTrigger + SplitText + Flip, Lenis, View Transitions API, CSS scroll-driven animations where supported. **Search current docs and caniuse before building, as the AB Solution CLAUDE.md requires.**
- **Hero, « Le rideau »:**
  - The dark velvet curtains (from the house photos) part via `clip-path` over 900 ms (expo.out).
  - The didone wordmark rises letter by letter (SplitText, 40 ms stagger).
  - The arc text turns into place along its circular path.
  - The sunburst mirror rays draw on (stroke-dashoffset).
  - The fringed lamp **sways gently** on a spring after load and tilts slightly towards the pointer. It is still on touch devices.
- **Scroll storytelling, « L'Enfilade »:**
  - A pinned section (`start: "top top"`, scrub 1) pans horizontally through **five rooms**, each a staged illustration with its real description: **Le comptoir** (tables & banquettes hautes) → **Les tables bistrot** → **Les alcôves** (grands coussins) → **Le pub anglais revisité** → **L'espace cave**.
  - Between rooms, a **doorway mask** (arched `clip-path`) scales up so the visitor "walks through" into the next room.
  - The light dims slightly room by room ("plus ou moins tamisées", from a real review).
  - A small progress line along the floor shows which room you are in, with room names as buttons for keyboard users.
- **The ardoise:** each Monday's mock "semaine" reveals line by line with a chalk-wipe mask (a left-to-right `clip-path`). A week picker flips the board like a hinged slate (rotateX, 600 ms).
- **Tesserae dividers:** mosaic tiles fall into a band as the section enters (CSS `animation-timeline: view()`, with an IntersectionObserver fallback).
- **The formula strip:** the prices 12 → 16,50 → 21 count up once in view; the didone numbers have an optical-size shift.
- **Agenda posters:** a slight tilt on hover (≤ 4°); a blind-test card plays a waveform shimmer; a karaoke card shows a bouncing-ball lyric line.
- **Page transitions:** the View Transitions API (cross-document, `@view-transition { navigation: auto; }`) with a **velvet curtain wipe**. The header wordmark morphs between pages as a shared element. Fallback: a 200 ms fade.
- **Micro-interactions:**
  - Buttons fill with the **cannage pattern** from the cursor's entry side (directional hover).
  - The Réserver button is magnetic (≤ 6 px; desktop only).
  - Focus ring: 2 px `--velours-clair` + 2 px offset.
  - Hovering « Mon œuf se met au vert » tints the egg from yellow to green.
  - Hovering a cocktail card adds rising bubbles (CSS only, ≤ 8 bubbles).
- **No custom cursor.** Both design-taste and accessibility advise against it.
- **Reduced-motion fallback** (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`):
  - The curtain starts open; the wordmark is static; the lamp does not sway.
  - The enfilade becomes a vertical list of five room panels (no pin, no pan).
  - Doorway masks become 150 ms opacity fades. Tesserae are pre-assembled. Counters show final values.
  - The lamp switch changes theme instantly with a 150 ms crossfade.
  - The cave becomes a filterable grid with "Voir la bouteille" buttons opening a dialog.
  - Lenis is disabled.
  - All content stays in the DOM; none of it lives only in a canvas.
- **Performance:**
  - Animate transform and opacity only, with clip-path sparingly.
  - Lazy-load GSAP modules per page.
  - Keep the first load light (the hero is SVG + CSS).
  - On low-power devices or touch, drop the scrubbed pan to snap-scroll.

### Copy rules on the site
- French typography: a non-breaking space before `: ; ! ?`, « guillemets ».
- **No em-dashes in site copy** (design-taste §9.G). Quote real taglines that contain "–" exactly or rewrite them with a comma.
- One label per intent: "Réserver" everywhere for booking; "Appeler" for the phone.

- Address **6 boulevard René Laënnec, 35000 Rennes**.
- Phone **09 81 75 93 50**.
- Instagram **@chezrene_rennes**.

- Every dish name and price in §3.1 and §3.2, dated "carte de la semaine du …, sous réserve".
- Taglines and the house copy quoted in §5 and §3.3.
- The legal-notice data (KARIOCA BSB, SIREN 980 808 307, capital €2,000, Nicolas Potier as responsable).
- GPS from OSM (48.10950, -1.66585).
- Hours exactly as the own site gives them, plus a "Horaires à confirmer" footnote until the owner confirms.
- Real review quotes **word for word**, attributed to the platform only.

**Can be mock** (flagged `isReal: false`, visibly "exemple" where it matters):
- Weekly ardoise archives.
- Cocktail, mocktail, G&T, beer and wine entries and their prices.
- Event dates.
- Group formulas.
- Journal posts.
- Gallery art.
- Job cards.
- Generic testimonials with non-name labels.
- FAQ wording, except for facts.

**Never invent or publish:**
- An **award** for the œuf mayo: no "primé", "champion", "lauréat" or "world-championship finalist". **Allowed (critic pass):** « Finaliste du Championnat de Bretagne de l'œuf mayonnaise 2026 » (actu.fr list), and "2025" only once the owner confirms it.
- Press quotes beyond the short owner quotes and facts read in the Ouest-France article (§2). Quote one or two sentences at most and link to the article; do not reproduce the paywalled body.
- A tourism-office recommendation.
- A **terrace or garden**, "café-concerts", brunch, DJ or spoken-word nights, or Saturday or Sunday opening.
- Delivery or takeaway.
- Real wine, gin, brewery or producer names; real cocktail names or prices.
- Seat counts.
- Staff names or photos. Do not tie "Alex" or "la Cheffe" to a person. The gérant's name appears only in the legal notice unless the owner agrees otherwise.
- Any mention of the **liquidation of SARL Les Bricoles**, or of the old Prévalaye venue's reviews, phone (02 99 67 23 75), Facebook or "600 références" (Le Havre).
- Sourcing from the Marché des Lices or a named producer.
- An email address.
- **The Novy Event photos**, unless the owner gives written permission.
- A redrawn copy of the real logo presented as official. Our type-set wordmark is an interpretation; flag it to the owner.

**JSON-LD:** `Restaurant` + `BarOrPub`, with name, address, OSM geo, telephone, url (current domain or GitHub Pages), `servesCuisine: "Française, bistrot"`, `priceRange: "€€"`, and `openingHoursSpecification` from the own site. **Do not copy the site's wrong JSON-LD coordinates.** No `aggregateRating` (third-party ratings should not be self-declared).
